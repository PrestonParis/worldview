# Georgia results: timeline to 100%

For the Nov 3, 2026 general election and the Dec 1 runoff. "100%" means three different things: every precinct in, every ballot counted, and results official.

| Date (2026)             | What happens                                            | What it means for the data                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wed Nov 4 (done)        | All precincts in, 4:18 am ET                            | Precinct totals are complete, but late ballots are still to come |
| Fri Nov 6               | Provisional and overseas ballot deadlines               | Last day ballots can arrive                                      |
| Nov 6–9                 | Counties process and upload late ballots                | "Fully reported" count should rise; timing is up to each county  |
| **Mon Nov 9, 5 pm ET**  | County certification deadline                           | **Every ballot counted, so 100% in practice**                    |
| Nov 9–13 (approx.)      | Recount window for locally certified races              | Burke Commission 4 (4 votes) could surface here                  |
| **Fri Nov 20, 5 pm ET** | State certification deadline; can come earlier          | **100% official**                                                |
| About Nov 24            | Recount request window closes for state-certified races | HD 103 (27 votes) and HD 154 (59 votes)                          |
| Tue Dec 1               | Runoff for 13 contests; polls close 7 pm ET             | HD 151 decides the last open House seat                          |
| Mon Dec 7               | Runoff county certification deadline                    | County-level runoffs final, barring a recount                    |
| **Fri Dec 18**          | Runoff state certification deadline                     | **Every contest final**, barring recounts                        |

Vote margins are from the state's feed as last updated Nov 4 at 4:18 am ET.

## How firm these dates are

- **Certification dates:** Nov 9, Nov 20, Dec 7 and Dec 18 are published in the [Brennan Center's Georgia certification guide](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/georgia-election-certification-processes-and-guardrails).
- **Nov 6 deadlines:** the provisional date is from the Secretary of State's 2026 elections calendar, read through a search excerpt because the PDF blocks scripts. State pages word the overseas deadline as both three days and three business days; both land on Nov 6.
- **Recount windows:** calculated from the 2-business-day rule, not published. The local window depends on when a county certifies and whether Veterans Day (Nov 11) counts.
- **Feed status:** the Secretary of State decides when its feed shows a county as fully reported or certified, which can lag a deadline. The results Overview reads the feed rather than assuming from dates.
